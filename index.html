About Me

Research-driven agriculture for a changing world

I am Md. Nazmus Sakib, a Graduate Research Assistant in the Department of Agronomy at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Agriculture from Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University with a CGPA of 3.88 out of 4.00, completing 182 credit hours.

My academic and research interests focus on the interaction between crops and their environment, particularly how plants respond to abiotic stresses and how improved agronomic and soil-management practices can contribute to sustainable crop production.

I am also interested in quantitative data analysis, molecular techniques, microbiology, bioinformatics, and NGS-based approaches that can support modern agricultural research.

Current Research Focus

  • Plant responses to salinity and waterlogging
  • Plant stress physiology and stress tolerance
  • Sustainable crop production
  • Soil science and plant–soil interactions
  • Climate-resilient agriculture
  • Crop performance under different nutrient regimes
  • Agricultural data analysis and interpretation
