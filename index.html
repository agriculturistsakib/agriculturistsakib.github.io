About

Hello! I am Md. Nazmus Sakib, a B.Sc. graduate in Agricultural Science from Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University and currently pursuing an MSc in Agronomy at the same institution.

My academic and research interests center on crop stress physiology, agronomy and sustainable agriculture. I am particularly interested in understanding how crops respond to environmental stresses and how physiological, biochemical and agronomic approaches can contribute to climate-resilient crop production.

My current thesis research focuses on assessing growth and physiological traits associated with combined salinity and waterlogging tolerance in wheat varieties, with the goal of identifying stress-resilient crop genotypes.

I also have research interests in salinity, drought, waterlogging, extreme temperatures, soil acidity, nutritional disorders and biotic stresses. My broader objective is to contribute to practical and scientifically sound approaches for improving crop adaptation, resilience, productivity and food security.

Alongside research, I have developed experience in data analysis, scientific visualization, reference management, laboratory techniques, leadership and academic communication.
